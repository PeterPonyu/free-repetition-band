4–10 epoch stratum

Plate I · The Band · PeterPonyu/free-repetition-band

Free-repetition field guide

Repeating training data is nearly free for four to ten epochs. The band is a memorization-onset stratum, not a capacity–entropy artifact.

Plate I · Band

Rfree 4–10

Range
Four to ten epochs across the core capacity × entropy grid. Does not move ~100× with the capacity–entropy ratio.
Census
218 runs · 20M-token budget · five capacities (2.5M–57M) · three entropies.
Diagnosis
Memorization onset bounds the band. Linear artifact account falsified; logarithmic reading not excluded at this resolution.
Field marks
WikiText-103: Rfree=4 (5/5 seeds, 3/3 draws). Source-code contracts to 2, returns to 4 after near-duplicate removal.

Plate II · Onset

Memorization onset

Copied-canary memorization coincides with decay onset in 8/9 cells of the core grid. The remaining cell (small/low) has decay at n=20 and memory at n=10. Summary: figs/summaries/E1_repeat.json via papers/FIGURE-INDEX.json or copied data/figures.json.

Probe
Canary gap threshold 0.10 nats; excess-loss free threshold 0.05 nats.
Ordering
Memory onset leads or coincides with decay; it does not lag once the fine grid is counted.

Plate III · Capacity

Capacity–entropy

The xl/xxl extension stretches capacity from 2.5M to 57M parameters. The free band does not track the capacity–entropy ratio. Summary: figs/summaries/E1_capxl.json.

Span
Five capacities (2.5M–57M) · three entropies · fixed 20M-token budget.
Verdict
Linear artifact account predicts a ~100× shift; the band stays in 4–10.
Material
E1_capxl · E1_grid · papers/figs/PIPELINE.md

Plate IV · Exposure

Band invariance

An exposure-matched control (plant rate 4 vs 1) shows that coincidence of memorization and decay onsets is an artifact of the oversampled probe convention. Rfree itself is identical in 9/9 cells. Summary: figs/summaries/E1_exposure_control.json.

Control
324-run E1-C battery on the same 3×3 grid.
Band
Rfree does not move when the canary is downsampled to match unique-token exposure.
